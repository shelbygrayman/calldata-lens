Keep in mind

    Inspect the ABI words

    A decoder, not a simulation or safety verdict. It cannot verify who controls an address or whether a transaction will succeed.

    UNDER THE LENS

    Small scope.
    Explicit answers.

    01 / MATCH

    Recognize the selector

    The first four bytes are matched against six common token function signatures. Unknown selectors stay unknown.

    02 / UNPACK

    Read the arguments

    Addresses, exact integers and booleans are decoded locally. Malformed or unexpected data is rejected.

    03 / CONTEXT

    Keep the unknowns visible

    A selector does not prove a contract’s behavior. Shared ERC-20 / ERC-721 signatures are flagged explicitly.
